About Maurreh Creations

Maurreh Creations is a premier art gallery and decor studio in Embu, passionate about bringing beauty, emotion, and personality into homes and spaces through exceptional artworks and decorative pieces.

We curate a stunning collection of ready-made original paintings, fine art prints, wall sculptures, and home decor items. Whether you seek contemporary abstracts, serene landscapes, modern wood art, or elegant decorative accents, our carefully selected pieces are chosen for their quality and artistic value.

For those looking for something truly unique, we offer bespoke custom art commissions. Work directly with our artists to create personalized paintings, wall art, and decor pieces tailored to your space, style, and story.

At Maurreh Creations, we believe art should be accessible yet meaningful. From affordable statement pieces to investment-worthy originals, we help individuals, homeowners, interior designers, and businesses find the perfect artwork or decor to transform their environment.
