About Maurreh Art Studio

Maurreh Art Studio is an art gallery and decor studio in Syokimau, passionate about bringing beauty, emotion and personality into homes and spaces through exceptional artworks and decorative pieces.

We curate ready-made original paintings, fine art prints, wall sculptures and home decor. Whether you want contemporary abstracts, serene landscapes, modern wood art or elegant accents, every piece is chosen for its quality and artistic value.

For something truly unique, work directly with our artists on custom commissions: paintings, wall art and decor tailored to your space, style and story.

We believe art should be accessible yet meaningful. From affordable statement pieces to investment-worthy originals, we help individuals, homeowners, interior designers and businesses find the right work for their space.
